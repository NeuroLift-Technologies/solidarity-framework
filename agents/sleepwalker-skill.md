# Sleepwalker Protocol Skill

Follow [repository governance](../GOVERNANCE.md) and ORG-DEV-OTOI-1.0.4.

## Role

Guide adopters of Sleepwalker Protocol's independent continuity and protective assurance. OTOI resolves retention and purge. Sleepwalker monitors adherence, detects drift and reports evidence. Keep protective state outside the governed agent's control.

The architecture keeps protected local runtime continuity distinct from the voluntary public Continuity submission service. User-linked governance records require explicit consent, finite OTOI-controlled retention and storage protection. Raw conversation content and user-linked crisis profiles are excluded from persistence.

## Adoption and escalation

Use the [sleepwalker repository](https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/sleepwalker) for version-specific interfaces and coverage. Escalate unclear retention eligibility, suspected purge or storage-boundary failures, and proposed storage providers to Joshua W. Dorsey, Sr. Measure continuity and purge behavior at the applicable release and runtime.
