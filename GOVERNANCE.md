# Governance for this repository

[NeuroLift Technologies' public governance contract](https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/.github) sets the required minimum for every contributor to Solidarity Framework. Repository-specific instructions may add stricter requirements within their scope and must meet the upstream minimum. The current baseline is **ORG-DEV-OTOI-1.0.4**.

- Read [How We Govern](https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Parse the canonical [YAML](https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/.github/blob/main/governance.yaml) or [JSON](https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/.github/blob/main/governance.json).
- Follow the repository-specific guidance in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).
- Use the [registration](templates/agent-registration.json) and [handoff](templates/handoff-record.json) templates when recording work in [docs/agent-log/](docs/agent-log/).

The local session and issue templates are imported from the [reviewed upstream revision](https://github.com/NeuroLift-Technologies/.github/tree/0890bdea5a2f91c37268eb8d851ccfc59f0bf1ef). The governance check compares their bytes with that revision and reports newer upstream changes for a reviewed update. Repository-specific coordination files are maintained here.

[NLT-DEV-OTOI.md](NLT-DEV-OTOI.md) records the earlier ORG-DEV-OTOI-1.0.3 contract and provides historical context for repository records. [ADR-001](docs/ADR-001.md) records framework architecture.
